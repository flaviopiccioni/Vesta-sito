Why Cortina

A growing market with two seasons

In 2025 Cortina d'Ampezzo recorded 1,081,186 guest nights, up from 994,456 in 2024, and it is among the top 20 municipalities in the Veneto region by guest nights (Veneto Region statistics, in Italian). In winter guests ski on the 120 km of pistes of the Cortina ski area, part of Dolomiti Superski; in summer they hike and ride through the UNESCO World Heritage Dolomites.

From 6 to 22 February 2026 the valley hosted the Milano Cortina 2026 Olympic Winter Games, which carried its name around the world. For an owner that means working with two different calendars: the snow season and the hiking season. In the mountains the service is also available in Cervinia, Valtournenche and Courmayeur.

Rental strategy

Short-term, seasonal or mixed: what works best in Cortina?

For most homes in Cortina, short-term rental with flexible stays works better than a fixed Saturday-to-Saturday week. With a rigid calendar, the most sought-after nights are the ones left empty: public holidays and long weekends.

That is why we set minimum stays season by season, rather than in seven-day blocks.

There are other routes too: a seasonal rental, with one tenant for the whole winter, less turnover and lower income, or a mixed formula, seasonal in winter and short-term in summer. The choice depends on where the home is relative to the centre and the lifts, the number of beds and the weeks you want to keep for yourself.

Regulations and compliance

What paperwork do you need to rent out a home in Cortina in 2026?

You need the tourist rental notice to the Veneto Region on the ROSS1000 platform, which assigns the regional identification code, and the national CIN code; then guest registration with the police at every arrival and, periodically, ISTAT statistical reporting and the municipal tourist tax. We handle it all, from the first notice to the quarterly deadlines, and the regional code and CIN are registered to Vesta.

Every step is explained in our guide to short-term rental rules in Veneto.

One detail that only applies here: the Municipality of Cortina has not joined any agreement with Airbnb for collecting the tourist tax. It must therefore be collected and paid by the manager on Airbnb bookings too, and we do it.

We register your home's regional code and CIN in Vesta's name. That is why filings with the Region (ISTAT included), the police and the municipality are our responsibility: if a requirement were forgotten or done wrong, any administrative fine would fall on Vesta, not on you, and you would never have to pay it first.

Tax management

What tax advantages do we handle for you?

Under the cedolare secca flat-tax regime, short-term rental income is taxed at 26%, reduced to 21% for a single property chosen by the owner. As the intermediary we act as withholding agent: we apply the withholding required by law and pay it ourselves, with no calculations on your side for each booking.

The rules are set out in the Italian Revenue Agency's guide to short-term rentals (April 2026, in Italian). Two points worth knowing:

  • the withholding: the intermediary that collects the rent applies a 21% withholding on account and pays it;
  • the two-apartment limit: from 2026 the short-term rental regime only applies if you use no more than two apartments for this purpose in a year; above that, the activity is presumed to be a business.

We help you choose the most suitable regime and, where the rules allow, treat part of the expenses as reimbursements so they stay out of taxable income. For your personal situation, always check with your accountant.

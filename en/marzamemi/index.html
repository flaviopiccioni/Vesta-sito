Why Marzamemi

What is the short-term rental market like in Marzamemi?

It is a growing market: in 2024 the province of Syracuse, which Marzamemi belongs to, recorded 513,795 arrivals and 1,473,984 overnight stays, 15.0% and 9.8% more than in 2023, according to the Sicilian Region's 2024 report on tourism in Sicily (in Italian). For an owner this means more demand, but also more listings for guests to choose from.

Marzamemi is not just about the beach. Nearby are Noto, Modica and Scicli, three of the Late Baroque Towns of the Val di Noto on the UNESCO World Heritage List since 2002, and to the north the Vendicari nature reserve: these are the reasons some guests also come in May and October, not only in August. The result, though, depends on how the home is presented and priced against the others in the area.

Together with Palermo, Marzamemi is one of the two places where we offer short-term rental management in Sicily.

Where we operate

Which areas around Marzamemi do we cover?

We cover Marzamemi and the coast around it: the village around Piazza Regina Margherita, Contrada Calafarina, the coast and countryside of Pachino, Portopalo di Capo Passero and the Isola delle Correnti. Our management also reaches Noto and the area of the Vendicari reserve.

The village and Piazza Regina Margherita

Homes a few steps from the square and the old tuna fishery appeal to guests who want to do everything on foot, from dinner to the beach. What counts in the listing is the location and quiet evenings in the months when the village is at its liveliest.

Contrada Calafarina and the Pachino coast

Outside the village, along the coast towards Portopalo, there are residences and villas with pools, terraces and parking. Guests here look for space and a view, and those are the points to put in the first photo.

Noto and Vendicari

Between the reserve and the Noto countryside, homes with a garden or outdoor space do well, chosen by guests who split their holiday between the sea and the baroque towns.

If your home is elsewhere in the south of the province of Syracuse, get in touch anyway: we assess each case.

Pricing and seasonality

When is demand highest in Marzamemi?

The strong season runs from June to September, peaking in July and August. May and October are the shoulder months, while from November to March demand is low.

The difference between a good year and an average one is often made in the shoulder months: a price too high in May leaves the home empty, one too low in August leaves money on the table. That is why we update prices every day with a dynamic strategy and work on minimum stays and spring bank holidays, such as 25 April and 1 May, to stretch the season.

Compliance

What paperwork do you need to rent out a home in Marzamemi?

The first step is the CIR, the regional code issued on Turist@t; with it you then apply for the national CIN. The CIR must appear in every listing, the CIN also outside the building. During the season come the daily reporting of guest data and the registration of guests with the police.

We register your home's codes in Vesta's name, so reporting and filings are our responsibility. The penalties are not small: according to the Ministry of Tourism (in Italian) they range from €800 to €8,000 for letting without a CIN, from €500 to €5,000 per unit if the CIN is not displayed or shown in listings, and from €600 to €6,000 for each breach on safety devices. With us, if something goes wrong, the penalty falls on Vesta, not on you.

What's new in 2026

What changes in 2026 for owners letting in Marzamemi?

The thresholds have dropped to two homes, both for national tax and under the Sicilian rule. From 2026 the short-let regime applies to no more than two apartments, and beyond that a business activity is presumed, as explained by FiscoOggi, the Italian Revenue Agency's magazine (in Italian), and in Sicily tourist letting remains a non-business activity only up to two units.

For those already letting, regional decree no. 1920 of 24 June 2026 required the Allegato B form to be sent by 30 June 2026; anyone who sent it without the full technical documentation must complete it by 30 December 2026, otherwise the activity ceases and the CIR and CIN are revoked. During the assessment we check whether this applies to you, so your home goes online compliant.

Tax management

What tax benefits do we handle for you?

We act as your tax withholding agent: we pay the cedolare secca, Italy's flat tax on rental income, and calculate it on your share, net of commission and costs, not on what guests pay. Where permitted, we treat part of the expenses as a reimbursement of costs, so they do not count as income and are not taxed.

For decisions that affect your overall income, your accountant's advice remains key: we give them a clear summary of takings, costs and taxes paid.

Personal use

Can I keep the home for myself at certain times?

Yes: in the owner portal you block the weeks you want to spend in Marzamemi, whether it is August or a spring bank holiday, and we only let the free days. You can change them when needed, and we adjust prices and availability.

It pays to block dates early, especially in July and August, when demand is highest and bookings come in sooner. If you want to keep the whole summer, we build the calendar around May, June, September and October.

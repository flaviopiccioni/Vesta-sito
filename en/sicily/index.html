Why Sicily

A growing market, with two different souls

Sicily is a growing and increasingly international market, but with two very different souls. The seaside locations have a concentrated, highly profitable season, where positioning and pricing in the peak weeks make the difference over the whole year. The cities instead have demand spread across twelve months, which calls for continuity of management more than the ability to catch a peak.

There's also a factor that weighs more in Sicily than elsewhere: over the last eighteen months the Region has rewritten the entire framework for accommodation, with a reform law, three implementing decrees, an administrative-court ruling and a corrective law. The obligations have changed several times and keep changing, and must be followed carefully month by month. Vesta manages them on the owner's behalf: from the regional code to daily data reporting, right up to the transitional-regime deadlines.

Pricing and seasonality

The right price at the right time

Sicily doesn't have a single seasonality. On the coast the season is strong from June to September, with solid shoulders in May and October thanks to the climate; the cities are urban destinations with year-round demand, where April to October adds a push rather than making up the whole season. You'll find the details on the dedicated short-term rental management pages for Marzamemi and Palermo.

With a dynamic pricing strategy we work both curves: we maximise the seaside peaks and keep occupancy high all year in the cities.

Compliance

Compliance handled by us

La Sicilia ha uno dei regimi più impegnativi d'Italia, e ce ne occupiamo interamente noi. In sintesi: servono due codici — il CIR regionale, che viene prima, e il CIN nazionale, entrambi da esporre; i dati dei soggiorni si trasmettono alla Regione ogni giorno, anche nei giorni senza ospiti.

The rules have changed several times in recent months and keep changing: we follow them, month by month, so you rent with peace of mind.

FAQ

Answers for owners in Sicily

Where do you operate in Sicily?

Currently on two hubs: Marzamemi, in the south-east, and Palermo with its coast. We're expanding our presence on the island: get in touch even if your property is in another area.

Is it worth renting out in Sicily with short-term rentals?

Sicily is a growing and increasingly international market. Seaside destinations earn a lot in the peak months, cities work all year. In both cases the result depends on management: we'll prepare a personalised estimate for your property.

How does seasonality work in Sicily?

There isn't a single seasonality: on the coast it's strong from June to September, with shoulders in May and October; the cities instead have year-round demand.

Who handles the CIR, CIN and data reporting?

We do: the regional CIR and national CIN registration (both to be displayed) and daily data reporting to the Region, even on days with no guests.

Do you also handle tax management?

Yes. We act as your tax withholding agent: we pay the cedolare secca ourselves and calculate it on the share that's actually yours — net of commission and costs — not on the gross. Where applicable, we record part of the expenses as a reimbursement, so it isn't added to taxable income.

How do I see my property's bookings and revenue?

Everything from our owner portal, with web access: bookings, revenue, available dates and your property's performance, in real time.

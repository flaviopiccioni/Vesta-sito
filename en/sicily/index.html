Why Sicily

How is the holiday rental market doing in Sicily?

Demand is growing and spreading beyond the summer. According to the Sicilian Regional Government's 2025 tourism figures, arrivals rose by 2.8% on 2024 and overnight stays by 0.24%, with positive signs in the mid and low season.

Seasonality also varies across the island: the same figures place Palermo among the provinces where it is least pronounced. International traffic is growing too, with flows to and from EU and non-EU markets reaching 36% of air passengers, up from 32% in 2023. That is why a holiday home in Marzamemi and a flat in Palermo need different management strategies.

Palermo

What should owners in Palermo know before letting?

Palermo has demand all year and a few local rules of its own, on top of the regional ones. The two that surprise owners most are the CIN plaque, which has a set material in the historic centre, and the city's tourist tax.

  • The CIN plaque. The City of Palermo has a local rule on the plaque: in the historic centre and on listed buildings it must be satin-finished burnished brass, engraved in black enamel; elsewhere plastic or glass are also allowed.
  • The tourist tax. According to the City of Palermo's tax office, it applies to guests not registered as Palermo residents, for up to four consecutive nights; the host collects it and pays it to the City within fifteen days of the end of each quarter.
  • Mid-term lets. Alongside short-term rentals, in Palermo we also manage lets of 1 to 18 months for students, healthcare staff and professionals on assignment, which keep the flat earning between short stays.

Compliance

What are the Airbnb rules in Italy, and what does Sicily add?

In Italy every short-term rental needs a national code, the CIN, displayed outside the building and in every listing, plus safety devices and police registration of guests. Sicily adds a regional code, the CIR, which must come first, and daily reporting of arrivals and departures to the regional Tourism Observatory.

We handle all of it: the CIN and CIR are registered in Vesta's name, and any penalty falls on Vesta, not on the owner.

  • CIR, the regional code. It is requested on the Turist@t platform of the Sicilian Regional Tourism Observatory.
  • CIN, the national code. Where a regional code exists, the Italian Ministry of Tourism requires it before the CIN is issued. Letting without a CIN carries a fine of €800 to €8,000; failing to display it or show it in listings, €500 to €5,000 per unit.
  • Safety devices. Every short-term rental must have portable fire extinguishers and working gas and carbon monoxide detectors, as the same Ministry rules confirm; the detectors can be left out only where there is no gas supply and no risk of gas or carbon monoxide. If they are missing, the fine is €600 to €6,000 for each violation.
  • Data reporting and ISTAT statistics. Reporting to the regional Tourism Observatory is daily and feeds the official ISTAT statistics; on days with no arrivals or departures, a daily closure must still be recorded. The same platform produces the file sent to the police through Alloggiati Web.

What changed in 2026?

Two thresholds dropped to two homes. Nationally, the 2026 Budget Law limits the short-let tax regime to two apartments per tax year: from the third, letting is presumed to be a business and the cedolare secca no longer applies.

In Sicily, regional decree no. 1920 of 24 June 2026 implements regional law no. 12 of 27 May 2026: tourist letting stays a non-business activity only up to two units, down from four. Anyone who filed the required form (Allegato B) by 30 June 2026 without the full technical documentation must complete it by 30 December 2026, otherwise the activity is closed and the CIR and CIN are revoked. The rules may change again: we follow every update and adapt your home and your filings accordingly.

Tax management

Tax benefits, handled by us

We act as your tax withholding agent: we pay the cedolare secca, Italy's flat tax on rental income, ourselves, calculated on your net share rather than on the gross amount paid by guests. Where permitted, we treat part of the expenses as a reimbursement of costs, so they are not counted as income and are not taxed. How this works by the sea and in the city is set out on the Marzamemi and Palermo pages.

Where do you operate in Sicily?

In Marzamemi, together with Pachino, Portopalo, Noto and Vendicari, and in Palermo, covering the historic centre and the coast at Mondello, Addaura and Sferracavallo. If your home is in another part of the island, get in touch anyway.

Can I reserve periods for personal use?

Yes, on your own from the owner portal: you block in the calendar the periods you want to keep for yourself, your family or friends. For the rest of the year we look after guests, cleaning, maintenance and compliance.

Who handles the CIR, CIN and data reporting?

We do: the CIN and CIR are registered in Vesta's name, and we handle daily reporting to the regional Tourism Observatory, ISTAT statistics and guest registration with the police. Any penalty for a missed or incorrect filing falls on Vesta, not on the owner.

How many homes can I let without running a business?

From 2026, two: nationally, the short-let tax regime applies to no more than two apartments per tax year, and in Sicily tourist letting stays a non-business activity up to two units. If you own more homes, we will work out together how to set up the management.

Can I use your service if I live outside Italy?

Yes. Non-resident owners can let their Sicilian home and opt for the cedolare secca; you will need an Italian tax code and you declare the income in Italy. We handle the management and act as your tax withholding agent, paying the cedolare secca on your behalf.

Do you also handle the tax side?

Yes. We act as your tax withholding agent: we pay the cedolare secca ourselves, calculated on your net share after commission and costs. Where permitted, we treat part of the expenses as a reimbursement of costs, so they are not added to your taxable income. The details are on the Marzamemi and Palermo pages.

Do you also manage mid-term lets?

Yes, in Palermo, with contracts of 1 to 18 months for students, healthcare staff and professionals on assignment. In Marzamemi we work with short-term rentals.

How do I see my home's bookings and revenue?

From the owner portal, with web access: bookings, revenue, available dates and your home's performance, in real time. Your revenue is paid out monthly.

About us

The Vesta team

Updated October 2026

Our team of 9 people works remotely and follows every property we manage, every day, with a dedicated role for each stage: listings, pricing, guests, administration. In the areas where we have properties, it works alongside local partners: local host, housekeeping and maintenance.

Remote team

A complete team, an expert for every stage

Each role covers a specific stage of management, for every property entrusted to us, wherever it is.

Property Manager

Handles the set-up of every property, its furnishing and amenities, and is the owner's point of contact throughout.

Revenue Management

Sets the pricing strategy and positioning on booking platforms, to raise occupancy and revenue in every season.

Marketing

Writes and publishes listings in several languages and channels, looks after copy and photos and follows up on reviews.

Customer Support

Assists guests before and during their stay and answers every request or issue.

Quality Control

Checks cleanliness and functionality with our local partners after every stay, so the home is always spotless.

Administration & Compliance

Handles monthly payments to owners, short-term rental paperwork and acts as withholding agent for the flat-rate rental tax (cedolare secca).

On site

Where we have properties, we have people on the ground

In the locations where we manage homes, the team works with trusted local partners, coordinated remotely every day.

Local Host

Welcomes guests when needed, inspects the home at check-out and is our point of reference in the area.

Housekeeping

Cleaning teams with a coordinator, linen included, ready for last-minute jobs too.

Maintenance

Trusted maintenance staff coordinated by our team, to step in quickly when something breaks.

How we work

Automated processes, checked by people

Most processes are automated with dedicated software and checked by our team, to work efficiently and avoid mistakes. Owners follow bookings and income from a dedicated portal, and always have a contact person to write to.

2. What data we collect and why (Purposes and Legal Basis)

Navigation data: The IT systems operating this website acquire certain data whose transmission is implicit in the use of Internet communication protocols (e.g., IP addresses, browser type, time of request).

Legal basis: Legitimate interest of the Controller (Art. 6, para. 1, letter f, GDPR).

Purpose: To respond to your requests for information or assistance.
Legal basis: Execution of pre-contractual measures adopted at the request of the data subject (Art. 6, para. 1, letter b, GDPR).

Navigation data is deleted shortly after processing (except where necessary for the investigation of computer crimes by Authorities). Data provided for contact requests is kept for the time strictly necessary to process the request, unless a subsequent contractual relationship is established.

4. Data Sharing

Your data will not be publicly disclosed. It may be made accessible to IT service providers (e.g., website hosting providers) designated, where necessary, as Data Processors (Art. 28 GDPR).

This website uses cookies to improve your browsing experience. For more details, please read our Cookie Policy.

6. Your Rights

Pursuant to Articles 15-22 of the GDPR, you have the right to request access to your data, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, and to object to the processing itself. You can exercise your rights by writing to info@vestadomus.it. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Data Protection Authority.

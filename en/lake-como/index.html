Why Lake Como

A destination with international appeal

Lake Como is one of the most famous lakes in the world, loved by international travellers for its elegant villages, historic villas, landscapes between lake and mountains, and its closeness to Milan and Switzerland. Demand is strong and spread across most of the year, peaking between spring and summer.

For an owner, this means being able to make the most of a home or villa in a highly attractive area, with guests often willing to pay premium rates for quality, views and location. Lake Como is one of the areas we cover in Lombardy, along with Milan.

Tax management

Tax management is part of the service

In an international, high-end market like the lake, a sound tax set-up matters to your return as much as the nightly rate. We act as your tax withholding agent: we pay the cedolare secca ourselves, calculated on the share that's genuinely yours and not on the gross paid by guests. We help you choose the most suitable regime and, where applicable, enter part of the expenses as a reimbursement, so it isn't added to the income you pay tax on.

Compliance

Compliance handled by us, with the CIN registered to Vesta

We handle all the obligations: in Lombardy the CIR comes first — a code for each individual unit, generated by ROSS1000 after notifying the Municipality's SUAP, which must be shown in every listing; then the national CIN. On top of that, reporting arrivals and stays on ROSS1000, notifying guests to the police and managing the municipal tourist tax. You don't have to think about deadlines or filings.

We request the CIN ourselves, registering it to the company: this way full responsibility for compliance is ours, not yours. You rent with complete peace of mind, with an orderly, tracked and compliant process.

FAQ

Answers for owners on Lake Como

Do you also handle tax management?

Yes. We handle the tax side as your withholding agent: we calculate the cedolare secca on the net amount that remains yours, net of commission and costs, not on the gross from guests. Where applicable, we enter part of the expenses as a reimbursement, so it isn't added to taxable income.

Is it worth putting a home on Lake Como to work with short-term rentals?

Yes. Lake Como has strong international demand spread across most of the year: with targeted revenue management, returns can be high, especially for properties with a lake view or in a strategic location. We'll prepare an estimate for your case.

Do you manage properties in Bellagio, Varenna and Menaggio too?

Yes. We operate in Como, Cernobbio, Bellagio, Varenna, Menaggio, Tremezzina, Colico, Lecco and the nearby areas most popular with tourists.

Do you manage villas, apartments and homes with a pool?

Yes. The service covers all types: apartments in the centre, villas with lake views, homes with a garden, pool or water access.

Do you handle international guests?

Yes. We communicate with guests in several languages and publish on international channels, to reach the foreign demand that characterises the lake.

Do you handle compliance (CIR, CIN and tourist tax) in Lombardy?

Yes. We take care of everything: the CIN request, guest reporting to the police via Alloggiati Web, tourist-flow reporting via Ross 1000, tourist tax management and rental obligations under Lombardy regulations.

Can I reserve periods for personal use?

Yes, fully autonomously from our portal: you block directly in the calendar the periods you want to keep for yourself, your family or friends.

Do you handle cleaning and linen?

Yes. The service includes cleaning, linen and property checks between stays.

Who takes care of my lake house when I'm not there?

Our team of 9 people manages listings, pricing, bookings and guest communication remotely; on the lake, local partners handle guest welcome, cleaning and maintenance. Meet the team.

Why Porto San Paolo

What is the short-term rental market like in Porto San Paolo?

It is a small but growing market, with longer stays than average: in 2024 the municipality of Loiri Porto San Paolo recorded 148,776 overnight stays, 17.69% more than in 2023, with an average stay of 5.05 nights against 3.31 in Olbia, according to the 2024 report of the Sardinia Region's Tourism Observatory (in Italian). Around it lies one of the strongest tourist areas on the island: in 2025 the Province of Gallura Nord-Est Sardegna, which the municipality belongs to, passed 7.6 million overnight stays (+11.52%), according to the Observatory's 2025 figures by province (in Italian).

The municipal figure is for 2024, provisional and extracted in May 2025: it is the latest published, because in the 2025 report Loiri Porto San Paolo is no longer among the thirty most visited municipalities. The provincial figure also covers Olbia, Arzachena and the Costa Smeralda, so it describes demand in the area, not in the village alone. In Gallura around six stays in ten are by foreign visitors, with Germany, France, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and the Netherlands as the leading markets, and among Italians more guests come from Lombardy than from any other region.

For owners, five-night stays mean fewer changeovers and a minimum stay to set with care, week by week. Porto San Paolo is one of the three places in Sardinia where we have homes under management, together with Stintino and Teulada: the picture for the whole island is on our page about short-term rental management in Sardinia.

Where we operate

Which areas around Porto San Paolo do we cover?

We cover the village of Porto San Paolo and the coast around it, with Porto Taverna, Costa Dorata, Cala Girgolu, Cala Finanza, Sconcatu and Punta Don Diego, as well as Vaccileddi and Loiri. A little further north, in the municipality of Olbia, we also look after Porto Istana, Capo Ceraso and Murta Maria.

This whole stretch of sea lies inside or next to the Tavolara - Punta Coda Cavallo Marine Protected Area, which according to Sardegna Turismo (in Italian) runs for 76 kilometres along the coast of Loiri Porto San Paolo, Olbia and San Teodoro, from Capo Ceraso to Punta s'Isuledda. But each area needs a different listing.

Pricing and seasonality

When is demand highest in Porto San Paolo?

From June to September: in 2025 these four months accounted for about 83% of overnight stays in Gallura, with August the strongest month and September almost level with June, according to the Regional Observatory's figures by province (in Italian). May and October together account for about 13%, April for less than 3%.

For a home in Porto San Paolo this means the real season lasts four months, not seven. With dynamic pricing we push rates in July and August, treat September as high season rather than the tail end, and in May and October we work on price and minimum stay to fill dates that would otherwise stay empty. These calculations also take seasonal facilities into account, such as the Sea Retreat pool, open from 1 June to 30 September.

Compliance

What paperwork do you need to rent out a home in Porto San Paolo?

You need the IUN from the Sardinia Region and the national CIN, to be displayed and shown in every listing, before welcoming any guest. Then, for every stay, guests have to be reported to the police, statistical data sent to the Region, and the tourist tax of the Municipality of Loiri Porto San Paolo applied.

The penalties are not symbolic: according to the Ministry of Tourism's FAQ on the BDSR (in Italian), renting without a CIN costs from €800 to €8,000, failing to display it or show it in listings from €500 to €5,000 for each unit, and each breach on safety devices from €600 to €6,000. IUN

How do you get the IUN in Sardinia for a home in Porto San Paolo?

If you entrust your home to us, we apply for the IUN and CIN and they are registered to Vesta: you don't have to prepare any paperwork. Private owners renting on their own must instead first send the notice of start of short-term rental to the Municipality's certified email (PEC), as explained on the Municipality of Loiri Porto San Paolo's page on IUN and CIN (in Italian), then apply for the IUN on the Sardinia Region's platform with SPID, CNS or CIE, and only after that apply for the CIN.

The steps are described by the Sardinia Region on its page on occasional rentals (in Italian): since 1 February 2025 the IUN can only be requested through the regional platform, and for the CIN you have to wait 24 hours after receiving the Ross1000 credentials before accessing the Ministry's database. Those renting as a business submit a SCIA on the SUAPE instead. If your home already has codes in your name, we discuss it during the assessment.

Tourist tax

How does the tourist tax work in Loiri Porto San Paolo?

The tax is applied by the Municipality of Loiri Porto San Paolo, under new regulations in force since 1 January 2026, approved by the Town Council on 23 December 2025. If we manage your home, we take care of it: collection, payments and returns on the Municipality's portal.

On the Municipality's tourist tax portal (in Italian), private owners renting on a non-business basis notify the start of the activity and pay through PagoPA, logging in with SPID or CIE; the same portal lists the exemptions. Rates are approved by the Town Executive and can change: that is why we don't give amounts on this page, and in the assessment we explain how they apply to your home.

What's new in 2026

What changes in 2026 for owners renting in Porto San Paolo?

From 1 January 2026, short-term renting of more than two units, even in different municipalities, is presumed by law to be a business activity, with the obligation to register for VAT. The Municipality of Loiri Porto San Paolo (in Italian) points this out, with reference to the 2026 Budget Law (Law 199/2025).

In Sardinia there is one more rule: for businesses, with more than two units in the same municipality or in neighbouring ones the rental has to be run as a holiday home (CAV), as the Sardinia Region (in Italian) states. If you have several homes between Porto San Paolo, Olbia or San Teodoro, before publishing any listing we check with you which case applies.

Tax management

What tax benefits do we handle for you?

We act as withholding agent: we pay the flat-rate rental tax, calculating it on your net share, that is on what is left to you after commission and costs. And where applicable, part of the costs is treated as reimbursement of expenses, so it stays out of revenue and is not taxed.

With a season concentrated in four months, income arrives almost all at once: keeping it in order from the first booking avoids finding out at the end of the year how much is really left. We send your accountant a summary of income, costs and taxes paid, useful for decisions on your overall income.

Personal use

Can I use my Porto San Paolo home for my own holidays?

Yes: in the owner portal you block the weeks you want to keep for yourself, your family or friends, and we only rent out the days that remain free. If your plans change, you unblock the dates and we put them back on sale.

It pays to decide early, because the weeks of July and August are the first to be booked. If you can choose, coming in June or in the second half of September and leaving August free, the month with the most overnight stays in Gallura, is the choice that makes the biggest difference to the season's result.

What we do for you

What does holiday home management in Porto San Paolo include?

It covers all the work behind a home for rent: listing and photos, pricing, bookings, guests, cleaning and linen, maintenance, reviews, paperwork and tax. In the owner portal you see bookings, revenue and free dates, and your revenue is paid out every month.

It is what many people call Airbnb management, but Airbnb is only one of the channels: we publish your home at the same time on our own booking site, Airbnb, Booking.com, Vrbo, Expedia and other platforms. For a home in Porto San Paolo the work on the listing starts from the questions of guests booking from far away: how many minutes' walk to the beach, whether there is a parking space, whether the pool is open on their dates. You can find every service, with what is included and what is available on request, on our short-term rental management services page.

Tax management

What tax benefits do we handle for you?

We act as withholding agent: we pay the flat-rate rental tax, calculating it on your net share, that is on what is left to you after commission and costs. And where applicable, part of the costs is treated as reimbursement of expenses, so it stays out of revenue and is not taxed.

With a season concentrated in four months, income arrives almost all at once: keeping it in order from the first booking avoids finding out at the end of the year how much is really left. We send your accountant a summary of income, costs and taxes paid, useful for decisions on your overall income.

FAQ

Questions from owners in Porto San Paolo

Which areas around Porto San Paolo do we cover?

We cover the village of Porto San Paolo and the coast around it, with Porto Taverna, Costa Dorata, Cala Girgolu, Cala Finanza, Sconcatu and Punta Don Diego, as well as Vaccileddi and Loiri. A little further north, in the municipality of Olbia, we also look after Porto Istana, Capo Ceraso and Murta Maria.

Do you also manage short-term rentals in Olbia, San Teodoro and Budoni?

Yes: as well as Porto San Paolo we serve the coast from Olbia, with Pittulongu, Bados and Murta Maria, down to San Teodoro and Budoni. We always start with a free assessment of the individual home, because demand changes a lot from one area to the next.

Who handles the paperwork?

We do: the IUN and CIN are registered to Vesta, and we handle police reporting, statistical data and the municipal tourist tax. Every step is explained in our guide to short-term rentals in Porto San Paolo.

Can I use my Porto San Paolo home for my own holidays?

Yes: in the owner portal you block the weeks you want to keep for yourself, your family or friends, and we only rent out the days that remain free. If your plans change, you unblock the dates and we put them back on sale.

Who looks after your home in Porto San Paolo?

In Porto San Paolo we work with local people, coordinated by us, who handle guest welcome, cleaning and linen between stays, and maintenance. Listings, pricing, bookings, guests and administration are run by the Vesta team: 9 people who work remotely and look after over 80 properties in Italy.

Why Porto San Paolo

The sea of Tavolara, in one of the most accessible parts of Gallura

We believe in Porto San Paolo, and for good reason: it's a hamlet of Loiri Porto San Paolo, in Gallura, facing the island of Tavolara and part of the Tavolara–Punta Coda Cavallo Marine Protected Area. Clear water, shallow seabeds and a quiet, family-friendly atmosphere make it a highly sought-after destination, more accessible and relaxed than nearby Costa Smeralda.

It's about twenty minutes from Olbia airport, which makes it a convenient base for guests and an area with solid summer demand, from April to October. For an owner, it means a seaside home with a broad, both Italian and international, audience.

Tax management

We optimise taxation to help you save

Tax is one of the most underestimated aspects of short-term rentals, and it's one of our strengths. We act as your tax withholding agent: we pay the cedolare secca on the net share that's yours, not on the total collected, net of commission and costs, and where applicable we reimburse part of your expenses. We help you choose the most advantageous option, to reduce the tax burden within the limits of the law and increase the net return that stays with you. Moreover, thanks to the setup we can offer you as a management company, you can pay tax only on what you actually collect. We assess the best solution for your case together, from flat-rate tax (cedolare secca) to the reimbursement of part of the expenses where applicable.

Zone servite

The beaches of Porto San Paolo and the coast from Olbia to Budoni

We manage properties in Porto San Paolo, overlooking the area's beaches and coves: Porto Taverna, Costa Dorata, Cala Girgolu, Cala Finanza, lo Sconcatu, Punta Don Diego, up to Porto Istana and Capo Ceraso just to the north. FAQ

Answers for owners in Porto San Paolo

Is it worth renting out in Porto San Paolo with short-term rentals?

Yes. The coast facing Tavolara, within the Marine Protected Area, has solid summer demand from April to October, both Italian and international. Its proximity to Olbia airport makes it highly accessible. We'll prepare an estimate for your property.

How do I see my property's bookings and revenue?

Everything from our owner portal, with web access: you keep bookings, revenue, available dates and your property's performance under control, in real time.

Which channels is my property published on?

On several channels at once: our website with a booking engine, direct bookings from our loyal clients, Google, Airbnb, Booking.com, Vrbo, Expedia, Agoda, Bluepillow and many more. More visibility means more bookings and better rates.

In which area do you manage properties?

In Porto San Paolo and along the whole coast from Olbia to Budoni: Pittulongu, Bados, Murta Maria, Loiri, Vaccileddi, San Teodoro (La Cinta, Cala Brandinchi, Capo Coda Cavallo) and Budoni (Porto Ottiolu, Agrustos).

Do you manage family or pet-friendly homes?

Yes. We work with both pet-friendly properties and homes suited to specific guest profiles, and we cater to recurring requests such as a cot and proximity to the beach.

How far is the airport?

Porto San Paolo is about twenty minutes from Olbia airport: a convenient base, more accessible than Costa Smeralda.

Who takes care of maintenance and property issues?

We do. We keep the property in top condition and step in for any issue, from a small leak or damp and mould to an air-conditioning breakdown at the last minute. That way the home stays cared for and fully working, for guests and for you.

What if a guest damages something?

We handle it: we open the claim and follow the entire process with the insurer to get the reimbursement and repair the damage, without you having to think about it.

Do you handle cleaning, linen, check-in and assistance?

Yes. We handle cleaning, linen, check-in/out and above all in-stay guest assistance, which is our strength.

Who handles IUN, CIN and tourist tax?

We handle it, at all three levels: IUN and SIRED/ROSS1000, CIN and Alloggiati Web, and the municipal tourist tax.

Can I reserve periods for personal use?

Yes, fully autonomously from our portal: you block in the calendar the periods you want to keep for yourself, your family or friends.

How do you manage the property if you're based in Milan?

We operate as property managers across Italy with a remote team and trusted local partners in every location, united by shared processes and technology: a real local presence, with efficiency, experience and professionalism.

Do you also operate in other areas of Sardinia?

Yes. Beyond Gallura we manage properties across Sardinia, from Stintino to Teulada. Visit the Sardinia page for the other locations.

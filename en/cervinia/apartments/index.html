Getting there

How to get to Cervinia

Breuil-Cervinia sits above 2,000 metres at the head of the Valtournenche valley, at the foot of the Matterhorn.

By car, Turin Caselle airport is about 120 km away and Milan Malpensa about 180 km. In winter you need winter tyres or snow chains on board.

Parking, garage and access to the limited traffic zone differ from home to home: you will find them in each apartment's description above.

Cervinia in summer

Summer in Cervinia

When the snow melts, Cervinia becomes a base for high-altitude walking: trails start a few steps from the homes and climb above 3,000 metres, with the Matterhorn always in view.

You can walk from the centre to Lago Blu, where the Matterhorn is reflected in the water, and the Golf Club del Cervino is a few minutes from the homes. On the Plateau Rosà glacier you can ski even in summer.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Are the apartments close to the slopes?

Yes, all of them. Cretes Blanches is ski-in/ski-out, Planet has the slopes right outside the building, Bich 16 is opposite the beginners' area and 240 m from the Cretaz chairlift, and Piolet 3 is 400 m from the Plan Maison cable car.

How does the ski pass discount work?

Thanks to our agreement with YouSnow, a sports association offering ski pass discounts, once your booking is confirmed we send you a code for each guest. The code gives up to 10% off the daily ski pass, bought online on the Cervino Ski Shop, the lift company's official shop, and is valid for one ski pass.

What time is check-in?

Check-in is from 4 pm and you can arrive at any time after that; check-out is by 10 am. Before you arrive, we send you all the details you need.

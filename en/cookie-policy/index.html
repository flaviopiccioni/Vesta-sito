2. What are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites visited by users send to their terminals (computers, tablets, smartphones), where they are stored to be transmitted back to the same sites during subsequent visits.

3. Types of Cookies used on this website

A. Technical Cookies (Strictly Necessary). These cookies are essential for the proper functioning of the website (e.g., saving language preferences or managing the session). They do not require user consent as they are based on the Controller's legitimate interest in providing a functioning website. If disabled via the browser, some parts of the site may not work.

B. Analytical Cookies (Statistics). These third-party cookies help us understand how users interact with our site by collecting aggregate information on the number of visitors and the pages visited. Their use requires your prior consent, which you can give or withdraw via the initial banner. For these purposes we use Google Analytics 4, provided by Google Ireland Limited, with IP anonymisation. The cookies set are _ga and _ga_<ID>, with a maximum duration of 2 years. More information in Google's privacy policy.

C. Profiling and Marketing Cookies. These cookies, generally from third parties, are used to track user navigation and create personalized profiles in order to show advertising messages in line with preferences expressed. They are activated only with your explicit consent.

4. Consent Management and Withdrawal (Cookie Banner)

Upon your first access to the site, you are shown a banner that allows you to choose which cookie categories to accept. You can change your preferences at any time by clicking on the "Cookie Settings" or "Review Cookie Choices" link in the website's footer.

5. How to disable Cookies from your Browser

In addition to our banner, you can configure your browser to refuse all or some cookies. Here are links to instructions for common browsers:

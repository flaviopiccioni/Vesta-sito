Why Valtournenche

The same ski area, without the altitude prices

Valtournenche is the village of the Matterhorn valley, with its main settlement at Pâquier. It has its own lifts connected to the ski area: you ski from the village and reach Cervinia. The area holds places you won't find elsewhere — Cheneil, at 2,105 metres, is one of the few Aosta Valley hamlets you can't reach by car, only on foot or by a small six-seater cable car, among larches and broadleaf trees; and the Gouffre des Busserailles, a gorge branching for 104 metres, with a sheer wall plunging 35 metres, its waterfall and giant's kettles.

For those who own a home here the point is precise: you're on the same ski area without the altitude prices. That changes both the type of property and the type of guest, and opens up sizes and price brackets that wouldn't work in Breuil-Cervinia. We already manage properties in Breuil-Cervinia, in the same municipality, and offer the service in Valtournenche village and in Courmayeur, in the Aosta Valley.

Pricing and seasonality

How is managing a home in Valtournenche different from Breuil-Cervinia?

Valtournenche, whose main village is Pâquier, and Breuil-Cervinia are in the same municipality and on the same ski area: from the village you can ski all the way to Cervinia. Prices and guests are different though, so the strategy has to target a different price range from the higher resort.

In Valtournenche, more accessible rates work well, with guests looking for an authentic mountain village and stays outside the peak weeks too. With dynamic pricing we make the most of strong periods and work carefully on the in-between ones, where positioning makes the difference.

Compliance

What paperwork do you need to rent out a home in Valtournenche?

In the Aosta Valley you first need the CIR, issued by the municipality after a declaration on the regional platform, and then the national CIN, as explained by the Aosta Valley Region. Guests must be registered with the police through Alloggiati Web within 24 hours of arrival, and guest nights must be reported to the Region for ISTAT statistics.

Unlike many managers, we register the property's CIR and CIN in Vesta's name. That makes us responsible for every filing: if a requirement were missed or done wrong, any administrative fine would fall on Vesta, not on you, and you would never have to pay it first and then claim it back from us.

We take care of it all, from the first declaration to the recurring deadlines. For every step of the regional code, see our guide to the CIR in the Aosta Valley.

Tax management

How does the tax side work?

As the intermediary we act as withholding agent: we apply the withholding required by law and pay it ourselves, so you do not have to do the maths for every booking. The rules are set out in the Italian Revenue Agency's guide to short-term rentals (in Italian).

From 2026 the flat tax on short-term rentals is 26%, reduced to 21% for a single property, and the regime applies to a maximum of two apartments a year. We set up the tax side before the first guest, not at the end of the year.

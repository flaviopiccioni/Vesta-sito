Why Sardinia

How is the holiday rental market doing in Sardinia?

The market is growing and the season is getting longer. According to the Region of Sardinia's BIT 2026 presentation, based on ISTAT data, the island recorded 21.8 million overnight stays in 2025, 15.6% more than in 2024, and 56% were by foreign guests.

Summer still carries a lot of weight: June to August accounts for around 63% of overnight stays, but April, May, September and October together already make up a third, and April grew by almost 38% on the previous year, partly because Easter fell on 20 April in 2025, compared with 31 March in 2024. In the same document, occasional lets, meaning homes let by private owners for short stays, account for around one overnight stay in five. A note on comparisons: since 2025 ISTAT also counts occasional lets, previously excluded, in its statistics, so the comparison with 2024 is not fully like-for-like.

Pricing and seasonality

When is demand highest in Sardinia?

July and August are still the peak, but the season does not end there. In the 2025 regional figures, September had more overnight stays than June, while between November and March the island got around 4% of the year's total. And it is not the same everywhere: in the places we manage, the season lasts longer in the north than in the south.

That is why we do not think only about July and August. Our revenue management works with dynamic pricing: higher rates in the peak weeks, and targeted work on May, September and October, when demand is there but has to be found.

Compliance

What do you need to let a home in Sardinia legally?

You need the regional IUN and the national CIN, both to be displayed and shown in your listings, plus ISTAT statistical reporting to the Region, guest registration with the police and, where the municipality applies it, the tourist tax.

With Vesta you do not have to handle them yourself: both codes are registered in our name, and if a filing is missed or wrong, the penalty is on us.

According to the Region of Sardinia's page on occasional lets, the CIN can be requested on the Ministry's database once 24 hours have passed since the IUN and ROSS 1000 credentials are issued, and the codes must be displayed at the entrance of the home and in online promotion; the Ministry of Tourism FAQs also require the CIN to be displayed outside the building. The same FAQs list the penalties: €800 to €8,000 for letting without a CIN, and €500 to €5,000 per unit if the CIN is not displayed or shown in listings.

Every short-term rental must also have portable fire extinguishers and working gas and carbon monoxide detectors; the detectors can be left out only where there is no gas installation and no risk of gas or carbon monoxide. According to the Ministry, if the devices are missing the fine is €600 to €6,000 for each violation.

What changed in 2026?

A national threshold has changed: under the 2026 Budget Law, the short-let tax regime applies to at most two apartments per tax year. Anyone letting three or more is presumed to be running a business and can no longer use the cedolare secca. When the rules change, we update your home's paperwork and filings ourselves.

Tax management

Which tax benefits do we handle for you?

Vesta acts as your tax withholding agent: it withholds the cedolare secca from your revenue and pays it to the tax authority for you. It is calculated on your share after commission and costs, not on what guests paid. Where the rules allow, part of the expenses is treated as a reimbursement of costs: it stays out of your income and you pay no tax on that part.

How it works in each location is set out on the Stintino, Porto San Paolo and Teulada pages.

FAQ

What do owners in Sardinia ask us most often?

Here we answer the questions owners of homes in Sardinia ask us most, from compliance to costs. If yours is not here, write to us: we reply within 48 hours.

Where do you operate in Sardinia?

We manage properties in Stintino, Porto San Paolo and Teulada. We also work in Alghero, on the Costa Smeralda, in Olbia, Villasimius and Cagliari; if your home is elsewhere on the island, write to us anyway.

What is the IUN of the Region of Sardinia?

It is the identification code the Region assigns to every home let for short tourist stays, and it comes before the national CIN. According to the Region of Sardinia, it must be displayed at the entrance of the home and shown in online promotion.

How is the IUN requested in Sardinia?

Private owners request it online on the regional platform, with SPID, CNS or CIE, after notifying the municipality in advance; businesses go through the SUAPE desk instead. With us, the IUN is registered in Vesta's name, so we handle the application.

Who handles the IUN, CIN and reporting?

Vesta, in whose name the IUN and CIN are registered. We also handle ISTAT reporting on SIRED/ROSS 1000, guest registration with the police and the tourist tax; if a filing is missed or wrong, the penalty is on Vesta, not on you.

Do you also handle the tax side?

Yes: Vesta is your tax withholding agent, so it withholds the cedolare secca from your revenue and pays it for you, calculated on your net share after commission and costs. Where the rules allow, part of the expenses becomes a reimbursement of costs and is not counted in the income you are taxed on.

How many homes can I let without running a business?

Two, from 2026: the Budget Law capped the short-let tax regime at two apartments per tax year, and a third one means letting is presumed to be a business. If you let more than two homes, in Sardinia or elsewhere, we will decide together how to set up the management.

Can I keep a few summer weeks for myself?

Yes. From the owner portal you block the dates you want to keep for yourself, for your holidays or for friends and family, without having to ask anyone. We manage the other weeks, from guests to maintenance.

Can you manage my home if I do not live in Sardinia?

Yes. The team works remotely on every property, and in Sardinia we are supported by local staff for guest welcome, cleaning and maintenance.

Which channels do you list the home on?

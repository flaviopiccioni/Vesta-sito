Why Teulada

What is the short-term rental market like in Teulada and South Sardinia?

It is a growing market, and private homes are growing faster than hotels: in 2024 the Province of South Sardinia, which Teulada belongs to, recorded 2,944,138 overnight stays, 16.71% more than in 2023, and stays in privately rented homes rose by 31.97%, according to the tourism figures by province from the Sardinia Region's Tourism Observatory (in Italian). Privately rented homes, like yours, alone account for about a fifth of all stays in the province.

Around four in ten stays are by foreign visitors, mainly from Germany, Switzerland, France, the United Kingdom and Austria. Among Italians the leading region is Lombardy, which brings more stays to South Sardinia than Sardinians themselves. For a home in Teulada this means a clear listing in several languages and a presence on the platforms German and Swiss travellers use, not only Italian ones. A note on scope: these are figures for the whole province, provisional and extracted in May 2025, not for the town alone.

Teulada is one of the three places in Sardinia where we have homes under management, together with Stintino and Porto San Paolo: the wider picture is on our page about short-term rental management in Sardinia.

Pricing and seasonality

When is demand highest in Teulada?

In the Province of South Sardinia about two thirds of 2024 stays came between June and August, with August as the busiest month, according to the Regional Observatory figures (in Italian). September, however, beat June, and between May and October the strongest growth on 2023 came in May (+46.40%) and October (+27.14%).

For an owner in Teulada this means one thing: summer brings in most of the result, but the extra margin is in May, September and October. With dynamic pricing we raise rates in the most sought-after weeks and, in the shoulder months, work on price and minimum stay to fill dates that would otherwise stay empty.

Compliance

What paperwork do you need to rent out a home in Teulada?

Before the first guest arrives you need the IUN, the Sardinia Region's code, and the national CIN, to be displayed at the entrance and shown in the listings. Then, for every stay, guest details go to the police, ISTAT statistics go to the Region, and the tourist tax is applied under the town's rules.

Penalties are high: the Ministry of Tourism's FAQ on the BDSR database (in Italian) list 800 to 8,000 euros for renting without a CIN, 500 to 5,000 euros per unit when the CIN is not displayed or missing from listings, and 600 to 6,000 euros for each breach on safety devices. With the IUN and CIN registered to Vesta, the penalty for a wrong filing is paid by us, not by you.

IUN

Who takes care of the IUN for a home in Teulada?

If you hand your home over to us, we file the IUN and CIN applications ourselves and the codes are registered to Vesta: there is no paperwork left for you to prepare. Private owners renting on their own first send a notice to the Municipality and then apply for the IUN online on the Sardinia Region's platform, with SPID, CNS or CIE, and only 24 hours after receiving the code can they request the CIN from the Ministry of Tourism, as explained on the Region's page on occasional rentals (in Italian).

Owners with a VAT number go through the SUAPE instead. If your home already has codes in your name, we discuss it during the assessment; the full picture of requirements across the island is on our page about short-term rental management in Sardinia.

What's new in 2026

What changes in 2026 for owners renting in Teulada?

From the 2026 tax year the short-term rental regime only applies to owners who rent out no more than two apartments; beyond that, the activity is presumed to be a business, as set out in the 2026 budget law and explained by FiscoOggi, the Italian Revenue Agency's magazine (in Italian). In Sardinia, moreover, three units in the same town or in neighbouring towns must be run as a holiday home business (CAV), started through the SUAPE and as an enterprise, as the Sardinia Region (in Italian) explains.

If you own several homes between Teulada and neighbouring towns, we check which case applies to you during the assessment, before any listing goes live.

Tax management

What tax benefits do we handle for you?

We act as withholding agent: we pay the flat-rate rental tax (cedolare secca) and calculate it on your net share, that is, on what is due to you after commission and costs. Where allowed, we also treat part of the expenses as expense reimbursements, so that they are not counted as revenue and are not taxed.

In a place where almost everything is earned in a few months, having the accounts in order from the first booking avoids surprises at the end of the year. We send your accountant a summary of income, costs and taxes paid, useful for decisions about your overall income.

Personal use

Can I keep my Teulada home for myself for a few weeks of the year?

Yes: you block the dates you want to keep for yourself, your family or friends in the owner portal, and we only rent out the rest of the calendar. If your plans change, you unblock the dates and we put them back on sale.

If you want your weeks in August, it is best to block them early, because they are the first to be booked. If you can come in June or late September instead, leaving August free, the month with the most stays in South Sardinia, is the choice that weighs most on the year's result.

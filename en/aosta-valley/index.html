Why the Aosta Valley

Two-season mountains, with complementary calendars

The Aosta Valley is a two-season mountain market, with a largely international clientele and demand spread across more months than seaside destinations. The region has complementary calendars: ski areas with one of the longest seasons in Italy and a strong summer at the foot of Mont Blanc, in Courmayeur, where the service is available.

For the owner this means more sellable nights per year and a revenue structure less exposed to any single month. Vesta manages the entire operational side and the regional compliance on your behalf.

Compliance

Compliance handled by us

In the Aosta Valley we handle it all for you: first the CIR, the identifier the Municipality issues through the regional platform and which must be displayed, then the national CIN. Every month we report arrivals and stays for ISTAT on the same platform, and we notify guests to the police within 24 hours.

Unlike many managers, we register the property's CIR and CIN in Vesta's name. That makes us responsible for every filing: if a requirement were missed or done wrong, any administrative fine would fall on Vesta, not on you, and you would never have to pay it first and then claim it back from us.

The tourist tax, governed by regional law and applied by the municipalities, is declared and paid twice a year: we manage it, from the first declaration to the recurring deadlines.

FAQ

Answers for owners in the Aosta Valley

Where do you operate in the Aosta Valley?

We manage properties in Cervinia; in Valtournenche and Courmayeur the service is available, each with its own page. Get in touch even if your property is in another part of the region.

Why is it worth renting out in the Aosta Valley?

It's a two-season mountain region with an international clientele: More sellable nights per year and revenue less exposed to any single month. We'll prepare a personalised estimate for your property.

Who handles the CIR, CIN and tourist tax?

We do: the regional CIR (to be displayed) and national CIN registration, monthly reporting of arrivals and stays for ISTAT, guest reporting to the police and management of the tourist tax, which in the Aosta Valley is paid twice a year. The CIR and CIN are registered to Vesta: any fine for a missed or incorrect filing falls on us, not on the owner.

Do you also handle tax management?

Yes. As the intermediary we act as withholding agent: we apply the withholding required by law and pay it ourselves, so you do not have to do the maths for every booking. In the cases provided for, part of the expenses is treated as a reimbursement and isn't taxed as income.

How do I see my property's bookings and revenue?

Everything from our owner portal, with web access: bookings, revenue, available dates and your property's performance, in real time.

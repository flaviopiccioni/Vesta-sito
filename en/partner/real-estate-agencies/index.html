The model

Vesta becomes your agency's operating partner

We put our structure, services and know-how in short and mid-term rentals at your agency's disposal. You offer them to clients who need them, without building anything in-house.

How we work together

Five areas where we work together

1. Properties for sale that are not selling

High price or a slow market: in the meantime we manage the property as a short-term rental, without taking it off the market. Viewings continue, the mandate is not interrupted and the property earns income.

2. Undecided clients

Owners who have not yet decided what to do with their home can put it to work with bridge management, with no long commitment. No client is lost while they wait: the agency stays their point of reference.

3. Clients who want to try short-term rentals

The agency can offer an extra service straight away, without setting it up in-house: we handle all the operations. One more reason for the client to stay with the agency.

4. Mid-term rentals, from 1 to 12 months

We also manage transitional leases, with the same operations as short-term rentals and a maximum length agreed together. Steady income for owner and agency.

5. Sales and sublets

Properties managed by Vesta that go up for sale are sold in partnership with the agency. The agency can bring us properties suited to short-term rentals that we lease and sublet, paying the rent to the owner. And properties with potential we offer to our investors.

Our commitment

The agency comes first

In every area Vesta gives priority to the sale and to the agency's relationship with its client. The client stays yours, and comes back to you for whatever they need next: buying another property, selling, a long-term let.

Vesta manages, the agency keeps the relationship. Every management is designed to bring the client back to the agency as their single point of reference.

FAQ

Questions from real estate agencies

Can a home that is for sale be managed as a short-term rental?

Yes. The property stays on the market and viewings continue: we plan the guest calendar to leave room for the agency's appointments. The sales mandate is not interrupted.

Does the client stay with the agency?

Yes. The sale and the client relationship always stay with the agency. Vesta only manages the property and keeps the agency informed.

Do you also manage mid-term rentals?

Yes. Besides short-term rentals we manage lets from one to twelve months under transitional contracts, with a maximum length agreed with the agency and the owner.

What does the agency need to do?

Offer the service to the clients for whom it makes sense and put us in touch. Listings, pricing, guests, cleaning, maintenance and paperwork are handled by us.

Where do you work with agencies?

In the locations where we already manage properties, by the sea, in the mountains, on the lakes and in cities, and we also consider partnerships in new areas. Tell us where your agency works in the form and we will talk it through.

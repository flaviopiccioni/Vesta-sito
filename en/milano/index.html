Why Milan

Demand all year round, with peaks from trade fairs and events

In 2025 the Municipality of Milan recorded 9,662,326 tourist arrivals and no month fell below 600,000: the lowest was January, with 608,226 arrivals, the highest October, with 924,399 (Municipality of Milan, monthly tourist arrivals, open data). So there is demand all year, but it is not a flat market: it moves fast and rewards careful, daily revenue management.

Milan does not have one tourist season: it has three that overlap. There is business travel, which brings people to the city from January to December; there are trade fairs, which create short, intense peaks, such as the Salone del Mobile, from 13 to 18 April 2027 or EICMA, from 3 to 8 November 2026, both at Fiera Milano Rho; and there are visitors who use the city as a base for the lakes. Milan is one of the places we cover in Lombardy, together with Lake Como.

Rental strategy

Short-term or mid-term rental: which is better in Milan?

It depends on the home: we decide on data about the area, type and size, cross-checked with how the properties we already manage nearby are performing. Short-term rental, up to 30 days, works on the peaks of trade fairs and tourism; mid-term rental, from 1 to 18 months, gives steadier income with fewer guest changeovers.

The 18-month limit is the one set for transitional contracts by the ministerial decree of 16 January 2017. Mid-term rentals suit professionals on assignment, expats, students, healthcare staff and families renovating their own home, and with us stays last 6-9 months on average.

If you have a preference, it counts: we show you the numbers for both options and decide together.

Regulations and compliance

What paperwork do you need to rent out a home in Milan in 2026?

You need a notice to the Municipality's SUAP office, which leads to the regional CIR code on ROSS1000, and the national CIN code; then guest registration with the police, monthly ISTAT reports on ROSS1000 and the Municipality's tourist tax. We handle all of it, from the first filing to every monthly deadline, and the CIR and CIN codes are registered to Vesta.

Tax management

What tax advantages do we handle for you?

We act as withholding agent: we withhold and pay the taxes ourselves, so you do not have to do any calculations for each booking. We pay the cedolare secca on your net share, after our fee and costs, not on the gross amount paid by guests.

The rules are set out in the Italian Revenue Agency's guide to short-term rentals (April 2026): under the cedolare secca the rate is 26%, reduced to 21% for a single property chosen by the owner, and the intermediary who collects the rent applies a 21% withholding as an advance payment.

Where the rules allow it, we treat part of the costs as expense reimbursements, so they stay out of taxable income. With short-term and mid-term rentals in the same city we also look together at which tax regime suits you best; for your personal situation, always check with your accountant.

Getting there

Getting to and around Milan

Milan has three airports and a metro network that links all the areas of our homes in a few minutes.

Linate is a few kilometres from the centre and connected by the M4 metro line. From Malpensa, the Malpensa Express runs to Cadorna and Central Station, and from Bergamo Orio al Serio there are direct buses to Central Station.

The Area C congestion charge zone, parking and car access differ from home to home: you will find them on each apartment's page.

Milan all year

Milan in every season

There is always a reason to come to Milan: Salone del Mobile and Design Week in spring, the fashion weeks in February and September, trade fairs in Rho and concerts at San Siro throughout the year.

In your free time you can go from the museums and churches of the centre to an aperitivo on the Navigli, and the train takes you to Lake Como in under an hour.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which areas of Milan are the apartments in?

They are in six areas: Navigli, Tortona and Giambellino; Centre, Cadorna and Sant'Ambrogio; Sempione, CityLife and Paolo Sarpi; Porta Venezia, Loreto and NoLo; Central Station, Porta Nuova and the north; Porta Romana and Porta Vittoria. Each listing shows the exact address.

Are there apartments for families or small groups?

Yes. Zuretti 1, Buenos Aires 35 and Bramante 15 sleep up to 6 guests, Meda 34 up to 5, and many others up to 4. Each listing shows the number of guests.

What time is check-in?

Check-in is from 4 pm and check-out is by 10 am. The latest arrival time differs from home to home and is shown on each apartment's page. Before you arrive, we send you all the details you need.

Any questions?

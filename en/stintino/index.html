Why Stintino

One of the most recognisable destinations in Northern Sardinia

Stintino is one of the most recognisable destinations in Northern Sardinia, chosen by Italian and international tourists for the sea, La Pelosa, the nearby beaches, Asinara, and the chance to enjoy a holiday in a home rather than a hotel.

For an owner, this means being able to make the most of a property that often sits unused for much of the year. Stintino is one of the locations we cover in Sardinia, along with Porto San Paolo and Teulada.

Self-management vs a manager

The difference between renting on your own and using a manager

Publishing a listing is only a small part of the job. A holiday home in Stintino must be well presented, correctly priced, precisely cleaned, inspected between stays and looked after during peak-demand periods. A property manager takes care of day-to-day management, while the owner retains control and visibility over the property.

Before listing a property, we assess whether it is truly competitive for the Stintino tourist market. Pricing and seasonality

Renting isn't enough: you need the right price at the right time

The Stintino market is strongly influenced by the season, beach availability, events, family demand and the behaviour of travellers who book early or at the last minute. That is why we work with a flexible pricing strategy, aiming to improve occupancy and the average value of bookings.

Compliance

We take care of the requirements to rent compliantly

Renting a holiday home in Sardinia requires attention to mandatory procedures and notifications. That is why we take direct care of bringing the property into compliance and of the operational management of the required obligations.

The owner does not have to handle the notifications personally: we manage them and take full operational responsibility, so the property can be rented through an orderly, traceable process that complies with the required obligations.

FAQ

Answers for Stintino property owners

Is it worth renting out a home in Stintino with short-term rentals?

Yes, if well managed. Stintino has very strong summer demand thanks to La Pelosa: with targeted revenue management, returns in peak months can be high. The potential depends on area, beds, distance from the sea and property quality: we prepare an estimate for your case.

Do you manage the home even when I'm not in Sardinia?

Yes. We manage everything remotely and on site: guests, cleaning, maintenance and compliance.

Can I reserve some weeks for my family?

Yes. We block the periods you want to keep for your family in the calendar.

Can I rent only during the summer months?

Yes. Many owners concentrate availability in the peak-demand months, keeping some weeks for personal use.

Do you also manage villas and cottages?

Yes. The service covers apartments, villas, cottages, detached houses and properties with outdoor spaces.

Do you handle cleaning and linen?

Yes. The service includes cleaning, linen and property inspection between stays.

Does my home need a CIN?

Yes. Online marketing of tourist rentals in Sardinia requires IUN and CIN, according to regional guidelines. We take care of the procedures.

Is there a tourist tax in Stintino?

Yes. The Municipality of Stintino applies a tourist tax for each overnight stay, up to a maximum of 30 cumulative nights. We handle the notifications and payments.

Do you manage properties near La Pelosa?

Yes. La Pelosa is among the most sought-after areas in Stintino: we manage properties across the area, optimising price and occupancy.

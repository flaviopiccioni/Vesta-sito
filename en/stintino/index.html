Why Stintino

What is the short-term rental market like in Stintino?

It is a growing market that speaks more and more languages: in 2024 the province of Sassari, which Stintino belongs to, recorded 9,737,727 overnight stays, 19.26% more than in 2023, and 57% came from abroad, according to the tourism figures by province from the Sardinia Region's Tourism Observatory (in Italian). The count also includes privately rented homes, that is, homes like yours.

Among foreign visitors the top markets are Germany and France, followed by the United Kingdom and Switzerland; among Italians, the leading region of origin is Lombardy. For a home in Stintino this means writing the listing in several languages and being visible on the platforms those travellers actually use. A note on scope: in the Observatory's breakdown the province of Sassari also includes Gallura, and the 2024 figures are those extracted in May 2025.

Together with Porto San Paolo and Teulada, Stintino is one of the places on the island where we have homes under management: you will find them all on our page about short-term rental management in Sardinia.

La Pelosa

How does access to La Pelosa work for guests?

From 15 May to 15 October 2026, La Pelosa beach can only be accessed with a compulsory booking, with 1,500 places a day, as stated on the official La Pelosa booking site. Bookings open at 9 am for the day after next, so guests need to plan before they arrive.

For anyone with a home here, this is information to give in good time, not once the holiday has started. In the listings of our homes near La Pelosa we already explain to guests how to book the beach, so they arrive prepared and do not discover the rule on the sand.

Pricing and seasonality

When is demand highest in Stintino?

In the province of Sassari almost eight out of ten overnight stays are concentrated between June and September, with the peak in August, according to the Regional Observatory's 2024 figures (in Italian). September recorded more overnight stays than June, and October more than May.

This means the season does not end in late August, and the shoulder months need as much work as the peak. We update prices every day with a dynamic strategy, run by a team dedicated to revenue management: we raise rates in the most requested weeks and work on minimum stay and shoulder months to lengthen your home's season.

Compliance

What paperwork do you need to rent out a home in Stintino?

You need the IUN, the code assigned by the Sardinia Region, and the national CIN, which must be displayed outside the building and shown in every listing. During the season come the statistical reports on guests, the guest registration with the police and the Municipality's tourist tax.

The penalties are not symbolic: according to the Ministry of Tourism FAQ (in Italian) they range from 800 to 8,000 euros for renting without a CIN, from 500 to 5,000 euros per unit if the CIN is not displayed or does not appear in listings, and from 600 to 6,000 euros for each breach of the safety-device rules. With the IUN and CIN registered to Vesta, if something goes wrong the penalty falls on us, not on you.

The Municipality is also paying close attention to the tourist tax: in 2025 it decided to support its tax office with outside professionals and to map accommodation, including rented homes, to bring unregistered ones to light, as reported by L'Unione Sarda (in Italian). One more reason to be compliant from the very first guest.

What's new in 2026

What changes in 2026 for owners renting in Stintino?

From 2026 the short-term rental regime applies to no more than two apartments: beyond that, the activity is presumed to be a business, as set out in the 2026 budget law and summarised in eDotto's guide to the new rules (in Italian). In Sardinia, anyone renting three or more units in the same municipality or in neighbouring ones must do so as a holiday home and apartment business (CAV), with an application to the SUAPE, as explained by the Sardinia Region (in Italian).

If you own more than one home in Stintino and the surrounding municipalities, we check which case applies to you during the assessment, so you start with the right set-up.

Tax management

What tax benefits do we handle for you?

We act as withholding agent and pay the flat-rate tax (cedolare secca), calculated on the share that is yours, net of commission and costs, not on the full amount paid by guests. Where applicable, we also treat part of the expenses as reimbursed expenses, so that it stays out of your revenue and is not taxed.

With a season concentrated in a few months, it pays to set everything up properly from the first payment. For choices that affect your overall income your accountant remains central: we send them a clear summary of income, costs and taxes paid.

Personal use

Can I use my Stintino home in the weeks I choose?

Yes: from the owner portal you block the periods when you want to be in Stintino, for yourself, your family or friends, and we only rent out the free dates. If your plans change, you update the calendar and we adjust prices and availability.

Our advice is to block July and August early, because those are the months when bookings come in first. If you want to keep the whole summer, we build the calendar around June, September and October: in the province of Sassari, September has more overnight stays than June.
